### Grainfather G30 (Version 3)

**Overview:** Premium all-in-one system with Bluetooth app control.

**Key Features:**
- PID temperature control with delayed heating (set up night before)
- Redesigned perforated-edge grain basket prevents stuck mash
- Rolled edge mash plate (no silicone seals needed)
- 6W magnetic pump for recirculation
- Counterflow wort chiller compatible
- 24-month warranty

**Pros:**
- Excellent app integration and recipe management
- Consistent results with minimal intervention
- "Brewing took half the time" vs. traditional methods
- Superb build quality

**Cons:**
- Premium price point (~$900-1000)
- 120V limits heating speed
- Proprietary accessories

**Source:** [Grainfather US](https://us.grainfather.com/), [DIY Distilling Review](https://diydistilling.com/grainfather-review/)

### Anvil Foundry 10.5 Gallon

**Overview:** Best value all-in-one for entry-level all-grain brewing.

**Key Features:**
- Dual-voltage: 120V (1600W) or 240V (2800W) switchable
- Triple-element, low-watt density design (prevents scorching)
- Double-wall insulation (±1°F temperature stability)
- 16 lb grain capacity
- High-flow grain basket
- Rotating racking arm

**Pros:**
- Exceptional value (~$350-400)
- 75-78% efficiency reported
- Brew day shortened by hours vs. propane
- Fits small spaces, ideal for indoor brewing
- Digital relay vs. manual switches

**Cons:**
- Lower efficiency than traditional 3-vessel systems
- 240V requires plug change
- Learning curve for first batches

**Source:** [Homebrew Finds Review](https://www.homebrewfinds.com/hands-on-review-anvil-foundry-brewing-system/), [Northern Brewer](https://www.northernbrewer.com/products/anvil-foundry-10-5-gallon-complete-electric-brewing-system-w-pump)

## Fermentation Temperature Control

Temperature control is the single most impactful upgrade for improving beer quality.

### Temperature Controllers

| Controller | Type | Price | Features |
|------------|------|-------|----------|
| **Inkbird ITC-308** | Dual-stage | $35-40 | Heat + cool outlets, ±2°F accuracy |
| **Inkbird ITC-308 WiFi** | Dual-stage + WiFi | $45-55 | Remote monitoring, app graphs |
| **Inkbird ITC-310T** | Programmable | $50-60 | Multi-step temp profiles |
| **BN-Link** | Dual-stage | $25-35 | Budget alternative to Inkbird |

#### Inkbird ITC-308

**The standard for homebrew temperature control.**

- Controls both heating and cooling devices
- Range: -58°F to 248°F
- Accuracy: ±2°F
- Displays °F or °C
- WiFi version: remote monitoring, push notifications, data logging

**Setup:** Plug controller into wall → plug freezer into "cool" outlet → plug heat source into "heat" outlet → place probe in chamber.

**Probe placement tip:** Tape probe to fermenter, then insulate with cloth/foam to measure wort temp, not air temp.

**Source:** [Inkbird](https://inkbird.com/blogs/homebrew/renegade-brewing-s-top-pick-for-the-best-fermentation-temperature-controller-for-homebrew-2023)

### Fermentation Chambers

#### DIY Chest Freezer Chamber

**The most popular homebrew fermentation chamber solution.**

**Advantages:**
- Space efficient (no shelves = more carboys/kegs)
- Energy efficient (cold air stays when opened)
- 15 cu ft fits 5-6 carboys
- Can hold steady 53°F for lagers
- Total cost: $150-300 (used freezer + controller)

**Setup:**
1. Acquire chest freezer (used is fine)
2. Install Inkbird or similar controller
3. Add heat source (ceramic bulb, seedling mat, or reptile heater)
4. Optional: Add small fan for air circulation

**Considerations:**
- Lifting heavy carboys in/out
- Taking samples requires opening lid
- Interior walls less durable than fridge

**Source:** [BYO: Build a Fermentation Chamber](https://byo.com/projects/build-fermentation-chamber/), [Homebrew Academy](https://homebrewacademy.com/fermentation-chamber-options/)

### Active Cooling Systems

#### BrewJacket Immersion Pro

**Self-contained thermoelectric chiller.**

- Maintains ±0.5°F of setpoint
- Both heating and cooling (Pro model)
- No ice, no water circulation needed
- Set-and-forget operation

**Pros:**
- Ferment ale and lager simultaneously (separate units)
- No external fermentation chamber needed

**Cons:**
- ~$300+ with insulation
- Difficult to take gravity samples
- Cannot see fermentation activity
- Fitting full carboy is awkward

**Source:** [Homebrew Talk Review](https://homebrewtalk.com/threads/brewjacket-immersion-fermentation-chiller-review.645556/)

## Digital Fermentation Monitors

### Tilt Hydrometer

**Wireless floating hydrometer for real-time gravity monitoring.**

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| SG Range | 0.990-1.120 |
| SG Accuracy | ±0.002 |
| Temp Accuracy | ±1°F |
| Battery Life | 12-24 months |
| Connectivity | Bluetooth |

**Pros:**
- No sampling needed—tracks gravity continuously
- Temperature-compensated readings
- Data logging to spreadsheet
- Multiple colors available for tracking batches

**Cons:**
- Bluetooth range limited (~30 feet)
- Requires phone nearby for continuous logging
- ~$135 per unit
- Krausen may affect readings temporarily

**Tilt Pico:** Bluetooth-to-WiFi bridge for remote cloud logging ($50).

**Source:** [Tilt Hydrometer](https://tilthydrometer.com/), [Hazy and Hoppy Review](https://hazyandhoppy.com/tilt-hydrometer-review/)

### RAPT Pill (Kegland)

Alternative to Tilt with WiFi connectivity built-in. Reported accurate FG readings.

### Plaato

**Not recommended.** Measures CO2 production and estimates SG via algorithm. Multiple user reports of inaccurate readings, especially for mead.

### Still Spirits Air Still (Countertop)

**Entry-level, no-assembly, plug-and-play distillation.**

| Model | Price | ABV Output | Capacity |
|-------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Air Still** | ~$220 | Up to 60% | 4L (1 gal) |
| **Air Still Pro** | ~$580 | Up to 90% | 4L (1 gal) |

#### Air Still (Original)

**Overview:** Revolutionary countertop still with air cooling—no water hookup required.

**Key Features:**
- Built-in fan cools and condenses vapor (no hoses, no water)
- Stainless steel body
- Produces ~1L at 38-40% ABV per 2-hour run
- True plug-and-play: no assembly required

**Best For:**
- Beginners exploring distillation
- Small batches and experimentation
- Water-scarce regions
- Essential oils and distilled water

**Reviews:**
- "REALLY a plug and play unit—put ingredients in, plug in, walk away"
- Recommended for triple distillation on small batches
- Easy to set up and clean

#### Air Still Pro (Upgraded)

**Overview:** Dual-mode still with reflux capability and automatic foreshots collection.

**Key Upgrades over Original:**
- **Reflux mode:** Produces up to 90% ABV in single run (vs. 60% pot still mode)
- **Automatic foreshots collection:** First 30ml collected in separate vial for discard
- **Built-in gin basket:** For botanical vapor infusion
- **Quieter operation**

**Specifications:**

| Yield | ~1.1L at 40% ABV per wash |
| Pot Still Mode | Up to 80% ABV |
| Reflux Mode | Up to 90% ABV |
| Dimensions | 42cm × 23cm × 36cm |
| Weight | 5.6 kg |
| Warranty | 3 years |

**Versatility:** Whiskey, vodka, gin, rum, essential oils, distilled water, vinegar.

**Upgrade Path:** Original Air Still owners can purchase just the Pro Head Unit to upgrade.
