### Kveik Yeastery (Authentic Multi-Strain Blends)

Kveik Yeastery is the only source for authentic dried multi-strain kveik blends, preserving the traditional complexity of Norwegian farmhouse brewing. Each blend contains multiple strains from the original farmhouse culture.

| Strain | Origin | Temp Range | Flavor Profile | Best For |
|--------|--------|------------|----------------|----------|
| **K.1 Voss** | Sigmund Gjernes, Voss | 59-104°F | Orange, tangerine, citrus; mild spice | Wide range of styles; traditional Norwegian ales |
| **K.9 Ebbegarden** | Ebbegarden farm, Stordal | 59-100°F | Apple, pear; tropical, citrus secondary | IPAs, NEIPAs, dark beers, sours |
| **K.14 Eitrheim** | Reidar Eitrheim, Tokheim | 59-100°F | Apricot, peach; orange, pineapple notes | Hop-forward beers; high flocculation for clarity |
| **K.22 Stalljen** | Stig Seljeset, Hornindal | 46-100°F | Tropical, mango, pineapple; orange, lemon | Pale ales, IPAs; pseudo-lagers (ferments to 46°F) |

**K.22 Stalljen** is notable for its exceptionally wide temperature range (46-100°F), making it suitable for pseudo-lager production at cooler temperatures.

**Dosage:** One 7g sachet treats 5-8 gallons. Optimal fermentation at 88-95°F completes in ~72 hours.

### Key Kveik Advantages

- Ferment 68-98°F+ without off-flavors
- Complete fermentation in 2-4 days
- No temperature control needed
- Can be dried and stored long-term
- High pitch rate tolerance
- Multi-strain blends provide more complex flavor than isolated strains

**Best for:** Quick-turn beers, pseudo-lagers, IPAs, NEIPAs, when temperature control is unavailable

## Usage Guidelines

### Pitch Rates

| Beer Type | Cells Needed | Calculation |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Ale (standard) | 0.75 million/mL/°P | ~150-200 billion for 5 gal at 1.050 |
| Lager | 1.5 million/mL/°P | ~300-400 billion for 5 gal at 1.050 |
| High gravity (>1.070) | 1.0-1.5 million/mL/°P | Use starter or multiple packs |

**Dry yeast:** Each 11g sachet contains ~200 billion cells when fresh. One pack is sufficient for most 5-gallon batches.

### Temperature Impact

| Factor | Warm Fermentation | Cool Fermentation |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Esters | More fruity | Less fruity |
| Phenols | More spicy | More restrained |
| Fusels | Higher risk | Lower risk |
| Speed | Faster | Slower |
| Attenuation | Often higher | May be lower |

### Storage

| Format | Ideal Storage | Shelf Life | Notes |
|--------|---------------|------------|-------|
| Liquid yeast | 34-40°F | 3-4 months | Viability drops ~20%/month |
| Dry yeast | Room temp OK | 2-4 years | Refrigerate for best longevity |
| Dry yeast (opened) | Refrigerate | 6 months | Reseal tightly |
