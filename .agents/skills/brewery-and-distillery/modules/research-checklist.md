# Research Checklist

Follow these steps to keep this skill current and accurate:

## Before Using This Skill

1. **Identify freshness risk areas:**
   - Yeast strain availability and characteristics (manufacturers update regularly)
   - Hop variety releases and specifications
   - Equipment recommendations and availability
   - Regulatory changes for distillation

2. **Run targeted searches for current information:**
   - Manufacturer websites (Wyeast, White Labs, Lallemand, Fermentis)
   - Hop supplier data (YCH Hops, Hopsteiner)
   - Brewing science publications (MBAA, ASBC journals)
   - Homebrew forums for community-tested methods

3. **Validate key formulas and calculations:**
   - Cross-reference ABV calculations with established tools (Brewer's Friend, Brewfather)
   - Verify IBU formulas against current industry standards
   - Confirm pitch rate recommendations with yeast lab guidance

## Validation Process

1. Check that guidance in [core-guidance.md](core-guidance.md) matches current best practices
2. Verify temperature ranges align with yeast manufacturer specifications
3. Confirm calculation formulas produce expected results
4. Update `known-gaps.md` with any outdated or uncertain content
5. Note confidence level adjustments based on validation

## Key Authoritative Sources

**Brewing Science:**
- "How to Brew" by John Palmer (homebrewing bible)
- "Yeast" by White & Zainasheff (comprehensive yeast guide)
- "Water" by Palmer & Kaminski (water chemistry)
- BJCP Style Guidelines (competition standards)
- Brewer's Association resources

**Winemaking:**
- "The Winemaker's Answer Book" by Alison Crowe
- UC Davis Viticulture & Enology publications
- Wine Science by Jamie Goode

**Mead:**
- "The Compleat Meadmaker" by Ken Schramm
- GotMead.com community resources
- TOSNA calculator (Meadmakr.com)

**Distillation:**
- "The Art of Distilling Whiskey and Other Spirits" by Bill Owens
- Homedistiller.org forums (check local legality)
- Artisan distilling resources

## Periodic Review Schedule

- **Monthly**: Check for new yeast/hop releases
- **Quarterly**: Review troubleshooting guidance against forum trends
- **Annually**: Full skill review against current publications

> Tip: Use web searches dated within the last year for rapidly evolving topics like hop varieties and yeast strains.
